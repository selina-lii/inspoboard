Quote card

A small object for a passage you keep: newsprint paper, dark gray serif text, and quotation marks as its frame. Right-click (or long-press) a card to change its marks. The marks row is one line in the menu.

Frame of the marks
App theme

Where it is made

Tool bar → Create note menu → Quote. A new quote card is 320 px wide; its height follows the text. Drag its corner to change its width and height (try the handle at the bottom right). A double-click writes the text. The card name is the source line, and it can stay empty (then no source line shows).

Marks

‘ ’ English · 「 」 Traditional Chinese and Japanese · “ ” Chinese · « » Spanish, French · none. The default follows the text: CJK text starts with 「」, other text with ‘ ’.

Data

One new optional field on the placement (for example marks: "cjk"). Old vaults stay valid; a card without it shows the default.
